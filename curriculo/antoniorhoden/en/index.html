Antonio Rhoden began as an art-finishing apprentice in Rio Grande do Sul at seventeen years old. There he understood how the magic happened: from india inks and airbrushes to cutting masks, from there to an enormous camera, from which a photolith was born. At twenty, in 1994, he moved to Rio de Janeiro, and already in the first months in Rio he received an invitation to draw a storyboard for the short film Chuvas e Trovoadas. He began creating pieces for cinema, such as posters and festival catalogs, and that is where he had his first printed work. The experience in cinema awakened another passion: photography and the art of seeing.


In 1996, after having learned restoration of papers and rare documents, he enrolled in the Museology course at UniRio, which, despite being unfinished, deepened his knowledge in Art History and philosophy. Already professionally involved in graphic production, from 1999 to 2002 he worked for Agência Formato, which served clients such as the magazine Brasil & Energia. There he did everything from image treatment and layout to the creation of advertisements and print supervision. During this same period, he also worked as art director of the electronic magazine 2k, a pioneering project of digital publications at the turn of the century.

In 2003 he was hired by RR Donnelley, which was beginning to serve the prepress market for art books in Rio. Major publishers brought experience, but it was in contact with Salvador Monteiro, of the legendary Edições Alumbramento, on the book Azulejos, that his more attentive eye to detail began to develop. There was born the feeling that every detail matters: the refined cut, the optical alignment and, more than that, the passion for printed art that reaches the hands of readers, recognized not by signature, but by the care of those who produce it.


It was also at RR Donnelley that he met Inês Coimbra, and the partnership between the two comes from there. In 2006, they opened together ô de casa edição de imagens, a bureau of specialized services in art books and layout. Major names passed through there such as Leonel Kaz, Andrea Jakobsson, Glória Afflalo and George Ermakoff, as well as publishers Sextante, Objetiva, Record, Senac, HarperCollins, Biblioteca Nacional and many others. Until 2014, over a thousand titles were published, and ô de casa became one of the main prepress bureaus in Rio de Janeiro.


With the closure of ô de casa, Giz Imagens e Letras was born in 2015: a small but prolific studio. Antonio specialized in adapting the design of foreign works, especially with handmade lettering, as in the edition of Eu sou as escolhas que faço (The Crossroads of Should and Must: Find and Follow Your Passion), work that earned him an invitation to also adapt the French edition, Choisir, c'est renoncer, from La Martinière Publisher.


The work with lettering comes from before. In 2007, Editora Intrínseca was looking for someone who could adapt the graphic insertions of the book The Book Thief, by Markus Zusak. There began a partnership that continues to this day. He then adapted the illustrations for the series How to Train Your Dragon, The Wizards of Once and The Terrible Two, among many others. He also lettered the adaptations of almost all the publisher's comic book editions, such as Nimona, American Gods (Graphic Novel), Black Hammer and Duna.

﻿

But it was with S. – The ship of Theseus that all this experience was put to the test. The book, conceived as an analog object, had all the margin annotations written by hand and, together with Inês's impeccable layout, arrived with the Brazilian public as a faithful copy of the original work. Still with Intrínseca, he signed the graphic projects for works such as Biblioteca Gaiman, Pan's Labyrinth, Coraline, Alchemised, The Ocean at the End of the Lane, Serpentário, Visão Noturna and several others.


Parallel to this trajectory, as senior designer at Editora Permanência since 2003, Antonio created and produced all books in the publisher's current catalog. The most prominent project, however, is the Missal Quotidiano, a unique book in the national market, in which he signs the complete graphic project and the redesign of over 170 engravings. From simple hand-sewn devotionals to the Missal Quotidiano, the books of Permanência carry the sensitive identity of a careful curation of its content. Learn more about Permanência editions in the dossier "Identity and Concepts in the Covers of Permanência".

He has also signed, with Inês, since issue 1, the prepress of the brazilian magazine piauí, which completed 20 years in October 2026.


Today he lives in Argentina, with Inês, his four children and the dachshund Luke.
